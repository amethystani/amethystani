# Support

Last updated October 2026

## Get in touch

Email [hello@playpip.io](mailto:hello@playpip.io). A person reads every one. If something is broken, say what you were doing and on which device, and a screenshot helps.

## Your progress

Your profile lives on your device. To carry it to another one, either add a free account (Settings, Your account) and it syncs, or move it by hand with a code, a QR or a file (Settings, Your account, Carry it across by hand instead). Neither is needed to play.

## Managing a membership

-   **Joined on the website:** Settings, Membership, Manage. That opens the portal, where you can cancel, change card or get an invoice.
-   **Joined in the iPhone app:** it is billed by Apple, so it is managed in Apple’s settings: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Pip’s own Settings has a button that takes you there.
-   **New phone, or reinstalled the app:** sign in to the same account, then tap Restore purchases on the membership page.

Cancelling stops the renewal; you stay a member until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds for a membership bought in the app are handled by Apple, through [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Deleting your account

Settings, Your account, Manage account, Delete my account and synced data. It deletes the account and the synced copy of your profile straight away. A membership bought on the website is cancelled with it. One bought in the app is not, because only you can cancel it with Apple, so cancel that first. See [privacy](/privacy) for what is kept and for how long.
